What a Calendar Is For

What other platforms put on a calendar, what a click on a day does there, and what that says about our rights calendar. In creator marketing a calendar means publishing; expiry almost always lives in a list.

Prepared by Julia, with ClaudeEvery image opened and checked18 platform groups (26 products), 8 with a calendar of some kind, 3 with an item calendar we hold a picture ofPart of Ninety Days, One Surface

The short version

67 verified images kept, 66 shown below. Every recommendation further down is PROPOSED by Claude; nothing here has been put to Tony.

  • A calendar is for publishing. Among creator platforms the only grids that hold items are post schedulers: Later (pictured) and Meta Business Suite Planner (documented in text, no vendor picture). CreatorIQ has a CALENDAR tab whose contents nobody documents. TikTok schedules with one date chip and has no calendar at all.
  • No creator platform is documented putting usage rights on a calendar. 0 of 15, with CreatorIQ's closed tab as the one unknown. Where an end date exists it is a column and a status chip: Billo (Expiration date, Expired, Extend permissions), Insense (Active for 33 days), CreatorIQ (Valid or Expired filter), Aspire (a filter and a saved view). At Social Cat, JoinBrands, soona, Influee and for Insense UGC the licence is perpetual, so there is nothing to plot.
  • The expiry calendars that exist are in SaaS renewal tools. Vendr (pictured), Zylo (vendor mockup) and Tropic (text only) put contract end dates on a month grid. Each one ships with a list twin and counts in days: "NEXT 120 DAYS", "Renewals within 90 Days".
  • A click on a day opens or creates. It never filters. Later opens the post, Meta offers Schedule or the post's options, Vendr adds or edits the contract on that date, Zylo shows a card. Filtering happens in chips, dropdowns and tiles outside the grid.
  • Deadlines are words and day counts, not grids. Insense "1 day overdue", JoinBrands "12 Days 07 Hours", soona's live countdown, Influee "4 days until deadline", Popular Pays "Due Feb 24". A month grid shows up only as a picker to set or move one date (Social Cat, JoinBrands).
  • The verb next to an ending date is Extend or Renew. Billo "Extend permissions", JoinBrands "Extend deadline", Collabstr "Request Extension", Vendr "Start renewal request", Spendflo "Renew Now". Nobody says "secure". Reminder lead times are stated by few: Vendr 120 days, Tropic 90, Tagger 7, Later Influence 3, CreatorIQ 48 hours.

What they do with a day

  • Click opens or creates. Later: click a tile to open the post, drag media onto a slot to schedule, drag a tile to reschedule, double-click an empty slot to start Quick Schedule, right-click to add a note. Meta Planner (help text): hover an upcoming day for a Schedule button; click an upcoming post for Edit, Duplicate, Reschedule, Move to drafts, Delete. No drag is documented for Meta.
  • Filters sit outside the grid. Later: profile avatars with check marks. Meta: two dropdowns, "Content type: all" and "Shared to: all". Both have a Week / Month toggle.
  • Today. Later shades today's column, draws a line at the current time and has a Today button; Share Calendar turns today's number blue. Meta's help text describes no today marker.
  • Past. Later keeps published posts on the grid (2 weeks back on free plans, 1 year on paid). Meta gives actions only on upcoming days and posts, so past items read as view-only; that is inferred from the help text, not confirmed.
  • Reminders. None tied to a calendar date in Later or Meta. TikTok sends one push when the scheduled video goes live. Post windows: Meta 20 minutes to 29 days ahead, TikTok 15 minutes to 10 days.

What they do with a day

  • Click sets one date. The grids here are pickers: click a day, then Save (JoinBrands) or Send (Social Cat). Later's Calendar Notes are the single case of deadlines on an item calendar, and a note has no status and no alert.
  • Today. A thin outline (Social Cat) or a pale green circle (JoinBrands) inside the picker. Outside pickers the countdown is the only "now": soona's ticks by the second.
  • Past and late. JoinBrands greys past days in the picker, turns the countdown tiles red ("Late to get content") and has a Deadline Missed toggle on the jobs table. Insense keeps overdue cards in the list in orange. Popular Pays shows a red clock. Tagger's current docs mark nothing as late.
  • Who can move the date. The brand alone at JoinBrands, Insense, Popular Pays (role-gated) and Collabstr (later only, never earlier). Either side at soona, and the other must accept. Creators at Insense and Later have to message the brand.
  • Reminders with a stated lead time. Later Influence: date-based templates, for example 3 days before and 1 day after the due date, sent between 7 and 8 AM Eastern. CreatorIQ: 48 hours before the submission deadline and before the publish deadline (a 2017 release note, read from an archive). Upfluence: N days after the last action, default 3, never a fixed date. Everyone else documents none: Aspire, GRIN (manual email), Tagger (event alerts only), Insense, Social Cat, JoinBrands (in-app timers), Billo, soona, Influee, Popular Pays (a digest), Collabstr.

What they do with a day

  • Click picks a start, then an end. Insense draws one continuous band across the range. Presets come first almost everywhere: JoinBrands (All time, Last week, Custom), Upfluence (eleven presets), Collabstr (Last 7 days, Last 30 days), GRIN's legacy dashboard.
  • Today. A small dot under the day (Insense). Traackr's chart marks it by switching from actuals to a shaded projection.
  • Past. Tagger changes the words: "Campaign is live ... until it ends on Mar 31, 2025" becomes "Campaign ended on ..." with a grey Ended pill.
  • Reminders. None.

Rules of the field

Derived from the evidence above. Each rule names the platforms behind it.

  • Rule 1. A calendar holds things you place on a date; things that run out sit in a list. Item calendars in creator tools are post schedulers (Later, Meta Planner). End dates are a column with a chip (Billo, Artlist), a stored field with a Valid or Expired filter (CreatorIQ) or a filter and a saved view (Aspire). Insense shows a status and a count of days, with no end date at all.
  • Rule 2. An expiry calendar never stands alone. Vendr has a Calendar / Table toggle, Tropic a "Calendar or List view", Zylo a calendar beside its Savings Pipeline list. The grid shows where the dates fall; the list is where the work happens.
  • Rule 3. A marker says what it is. Vendr: logo, name and value on the day, then "1 more". Later: thumbnail and time. Zylo tints the day and names the contract in a hover card. Nobody leaves an anonymous dot with no way to read it.
  • Rule 4. Today is marked and there is a way back to it. A Today button in Later and Vendr; a filled circle (Vendr), a blue number (Later), an outline (Social Cat), a dot (Insense), a pale circle (JoinBrands). Days outside the month are greyed inside the grid (Later, Insense, Social Cat). Past days are greyed only in pickers (JoinBrands), not in item calendars (Vendr).
  • Rule 5. A click on a day opens or creates; filters live outside the grid. Opens: Later, Meta Planner, Vendr, Zylo. Filters: Meta's two dropdowns, Later's profile avatars, Vendr's chips and saved views, Tropic's tiles, Aspire's saved view. No platform in the set uses a day as a filter for a second list.
  • Rule 6. Urgency is said in words and days; colour comes second. "1 day overdue" (Insense), "Late to get content" over red tiles (JoinBrands), "4 days until deadline" (Influee), "Due Feb 24" with a red clock (Popular Pays), "Expired" (Billo), "NEXT 120 DAYS" (Vendr), "within 90 Days" (Tropic).
  • Rule 7. The action sits on the row next to the date, and the verb is Extend or Renew. "Extend permissions" (Billo), "Extend deadline" (JoinBrands), "Request Extension" (Collabstr), "Propose new due date" (soona), "Start renewal request" (Vendr), "Renew Now" (Spendflo), "Change Date" (Tropic). Artlist is the exception on placement: "Turn renewal on" sits in the account menu.
  • Rule 8. Few say when they will remind you, and those who do count in days before. Vendr 120 days, Tropic 90, Tagger 7, Later Influence 3, CreatorIQ 48 hours. Billo tells you only once it has expired. Most creator platforms document nothing.

What this means for the rights calendar
