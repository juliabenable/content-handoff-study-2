# Ninety Days, One Surface

Study and prototype for the Benable Brand Portal: where a brand downloads each creator's video, sees when its usage rights end, and offers the creator more time.

- `index.html`: the study page. What came out of Tony's Oct 2 review and Julia's Oct 5 notes, tagged Agreed, Proposed or Open with who and when.
- `surface.html`: the clickable prototype (Rights tab, Content tab with a three-dot menu, extend sheet). The bar at the bottom switches the day, switches offers per creator or per video, and lets a creator answer a pending offer with yes, a counter-offer or no.
- `surface.before-tony-oct2.html`: the version Tony reviewed on Oct 2.
- `calendar-study/`: what other platforms use a calendar for.

Demo footage and creator names are fictional. Working notes and research stay in the local folder and are not part of this published copy.
